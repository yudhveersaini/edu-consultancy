Our Story

Hum jaante hain, admission sirf ek form nahi.

2014 mein hamare founder ne dekha ki kai talented students galat jaankari, agents ke fake promises aur last-minute confusion ki wajah se apni pasand ka college kho dete hain. EduVista isi gap ko bharne ke liye bana.

Aaj hamari team mein experienced career counsellors, ex-admission officers aur study-abroad specialists hain, jo har student ke liye ek personal roadmap banate hain — ethics aur transparency ke saath.

  • Step-by-step documented counselling process
  • Har student ke liye dedicated relationship manager
  • Parents ke liye regular progress updates (WhatsApp + call)
  • Post-admission support — hostel, travel aur orientation
